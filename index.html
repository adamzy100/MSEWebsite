Who We Are

A leading scaffolding contractor, built on quality and safety

Established in 2010, Multi Scaff Engineering Pte Ltd has steadily grown into one of Singapore's trusted scaffolding contractors, supporting major construction projects, infrastructure works and building upgrading and maintenance across the island.

We operate as a dedicated scaffolding specialist, delivering quality work with safety, reliability, and a firm commitment to on-budget, on-schedule outcomes that meet our clients' satisfaction.

Quality Workmanship

Every scaffold designed, calculated and built to the highest standard.

Safety & Compliance

Performed by a competent, qualified workforce under strict safety protocols.

On-Budget, On-Schedule

Reliable delivery dates that keep the wider project on track.

What We Do

Scaffolding services, start to finish

We operate as a dedicated scaffolding contractor providing full services, with all materials and labour provisioned by our business directly.

Design & Calculation

Engineering design and structural calculation for every scaffold configuration, tailored to the site and the works above.

Statutory Submissions

Preparation and submission of scaffold plans and documentation to meet Singapore's regulatory requirements.

Installation & Dismantling

Efficient, safety-first erection and dismantling crews mobilised for projects of any scale, anywhere in Singapore.

Hoarding

Site hoarding design and installation, providing secure and compliant perimeter protection for works in progress.

Our Work

Projects across Singapore

A selection of scaffolding projects delivered for airports, heritage buildings, transit stations, industrial plants and commercial towers.
